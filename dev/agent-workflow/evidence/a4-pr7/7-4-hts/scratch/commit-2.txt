A4-PR7 §7.4 (C-tools+harness 2/5): migrate the D4 restart/reopen harness Blueprint to v3

packages/tools/harness/d4-restart-reopen.mjs embeds its boot document as
`const BLUEPRINT_DOC` and hands it to the production row (`:740 blueprintSource:
BLUEPRINT_DOC`); the host parses that source at boot (src/plugin/host.ts:2125
classifyBlueprintAnchor → blueprint-authority.ts:328, and :1857-1862
createBlueprintSourceIndex). The version is therefore read by the product, so the
fence's `dirty` verdict is right and the disposition is migrate-by-hand: after
§7.3's flip this harness would not boot at all on a v1 document.

v3 requires both authority documents and defaults neither
(domain/blueprint/src/validate.ts:1335-1336). Both are carried as `rules: []`:
the narrowest legal value and the exact posture this team always had — the v1
document carried neither carrier, an absent carrier is a typed absence meaning
zero expansion authority, and the reader folds absent and declared-empty into the
same ceiling. D4 proves that a durable team root survives a host restart and
reopens with the closed wire row shape; it opens no permission expansion, so any
rule written here would be authority the harness never exercises. Every other
line of the document is byte-identical, and the fence, its scope and its version
constants were not touched.
