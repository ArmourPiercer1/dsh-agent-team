## 0. User-ruled contract (the settled model)

Request (verbatim): 「请你修改契约，允许exec类工具（bash, pwsh）放入leader的
allow与mutation envelop」— settled through clarification rounds:

1. **Leader template** `capabilities.permissions.allow` MAY carry
   `{tool: bash|pwsh, resource: {kind: any}}` — an EXPLICIT declaration
   only. NO implicit default-allow: a blueprint without the rule still
   resolves to `policy.default` (ask). **Member templates unchanged**
   (A2C-1 stands: member allow-lane shell-class rejection, diagnostics
   byte-identical). `exact`/`subtree` shell resources stay rejected in
   EVERY lane of EVERY role.
2. **Dual gate (双闸强制, user-selected)**: a leader exec ALLOW
   additionally requires the leader's effective mutation envelope to
   carry the matching token; missing → the allow DOWNGRADES to the
   `user-approval` ask path (human decides; zero effect until approval).
   `'bash'` / `'pwsh'` are SEPARATE envelope tokens — `bash authority
   != pwsh authority` (separate rules, separate tokens, separate
   fingerprints).
3. `'bash'`/`'pwsh'` become recognized exec-authorization slots in the
   envelope vocabulary (parse layer unchanged — open slug grammar).
4. Member routing UNCHANGED (verified, not modified): member ask →
   `leader-approval` → resolvers {leader, human}.
