Why route-C is not the old path here
------------------------------------
The Stage-0 probe measured that the product route-C attach appends no
agent-control shadow: all seven dispatches are `unknown tool` refusals with zero
provider calls and zero guard facts. A route-C fixture would therefore have
recorded the HOST's native rows, not the governed family being retired. The old
path for this family is the governed family body at C1. See
dev/agent-workflow/evidence/ute3-agentctl-b1-20261121/composition-probe.txt.

What this fixture is NOT
------------------------
It is not a boot, not a product switch and not a safety verdict. It proves what
the governed family body DOES at C1 for seven members, and nothing about the
native host rows' own encoding (README.md, UNKNOWNs).
